Privacy Policy for TalonTour

Last updated: November 13, 2026

TalonTour is a travel application designed to help users explore Thailand and plan trips.

Location Information

With your permission, TalonTour accesses your device location to provide nearby places, map searches, and navigation features. Location access can be controlled through your device settings.

Maps and Navigation

TalonTour uses Apple Maps and MapKit. Map searches and navigation requests may be processed by Apple according to Apple's privacy policies.

Advertising

TalonTour may display advertisements through Google AdMob. Google and its partners may process device identifiers, advertising data, and other information as described in Google's privacy policy. Where required, users will be offered appropriate privacy choices.

In-App Purchases

TalonTour may offer optional in-app purchases processed through Apple's App Store. Payment information is handled by Apple.

Local Data Storage

The app may store user preferences, favorites, and saved trip plans locally on the device.

Data Security

We aim to protect user information. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Children's Privacy

The app is not specifically directed to children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when application features or data practices change.
